How timing works

Banner: slide down → stay visible → slide up → wait for the interval → next banner. Upload 1280 × 128 artwork; it stays centred and keeps its 10:1 ratio.

Logos: leave rotation off for a fixed logo. Enable rotation to fade in, hold, fade out, optionally wait, then show the next logo. One logo can also fade in and out repeatedly.

Each layer has its own settings and playlist. Settings and uploaded images are saved on this server.
